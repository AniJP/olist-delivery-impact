# Phase 3: How long will this order take?

Full analysis: [`analysis/03_delivery_time_model.ipynb`](../analysis/03_delivery_time_model.ipynb). Model code: [`analysis/predict.py`](../analysis/predict.py). Features: [`fct_order_features`](../transform/models/marts/fct_order_features.sql). Coverage table: [`phase3_coverage.csv`](phase3_coverage.csv).

## Goal
- For each order, at checkout, predict the distribution of delivery time in calendar days. The model predicts quantiles P50 to P97.5.
- A promise set at the q-th quantile should be kept for a share q of orders. That turns the model into a dial for the reliability-vs-speed trade-off (Phase 4).

## Features (all known at checkout)
29 features in `marts.fct_order_features`:
- **Order:** route (seller state → customer state), distance, basket size, value, freight, weight, volume, category, payment.
- **Incumbent signal:** Olist's own promised days.
- **Seller track record:** prior delivered orders, average delivery days, days to ship, late rate, time since last delivery.
- **Network congestion:**
  - delivery time and late rate over the previous 14 days
  - purchases over the previous 7 days
  - open (undelivered) orders
  - delivery time to the destination state over the previous 30 days

**Leakage guards in dbt:**
- A unit test checks that a seller's history ignores orders still in transit at checkout.
- A singular test checks that no order's seller history counts more orders than the seller had sold before it.
- A second singular test recomputes the 14-day congestion window independently.

## Validation design
- **Train:** Jan–Dec 2017, 43,937 orders.
- **Calibrate:** Jan 2018, 7,143 orders. Used for early stopping and the conformal offsets.
- **Test:** Feb–Aug 2018, 46,036 orders. This period includes the Feb–Mar 2018 disruption, followed by much faster deliveries.
- **Never-delivered orders:** 493 test orders were still "shipped" at extraction. They are kept and count as missed promises.

**At Olist's own test-period on-time rate (91.0%):**

| Method | Avg promise | vs Olist |
|---|---|---|
| Olist | 22.9 days | |
| Route quantiles + conformal | 22.2 days | −0.75 days |
| LightGBM + conformal | **20.9 days** | **−2.0 days (−9%)** |

**P(late) at Olist's actual promise:**
- AUC 0.74; Brier score 0.078 vs 0.082 for always predicting the base rate.
- The riskiest tenth of orders was late 27% of the time, against 1.5% for the safest tenth.
- Predicted levels run high (35% vs 27% in the top decile) because deliveries sped up after the calibration month.

## Limitations and next steps
- **Disruptions:** neither Olist nor the model anticipated Feb–Mar 2018. Recommended operations:
  - refresh the conformal offsets weekly from recently delivered orders
  - alert when the live on-time rate drops
- **Drift:** lower quantiles over-cover after June 2018, because deliveries got faster than anything in the training data. Periodic retraining would sharpen promises further.
- **Undelivered orders:** about 1% of orders never arrive, which caps the achievable on-time rate at about 99%.
